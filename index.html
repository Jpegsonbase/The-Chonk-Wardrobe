About Daily Chonk

What if your Chonk got a new outfit every day?

Daily Chonk is an onchain experiment that gives a Chonk a fresh look every 24 hours. A bot randomly selects from a wardrobe made up of the original base traits, mixed with a growing collection of face, head, and accessory traits — creating a new combination every day.

No manual outfit changes. No two days are quite the same. Just one Chonk, getting dressed up and doing its thing.

Check back tomorrow. The Chonk will be wearing something new.
