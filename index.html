The experiment

One Chonk. One new fit every day.

What if your Chonk got a new outfit every day?

Daily Chonk is an onchain experiment that makes the most out of its ERC-6551 backpack. A bot randomly selects from a wardrobe made up of the original base traits, mixed with a growing collection of face, head, and accessory traits, creating a new combination every day.

No manual outfit changes. No two days are quite the same. Just one Chonk, getting dressed up and doing its thing.

New day. New fit. Same Chonk.
